# Nexus Health Report

The health report is generated by `scripts/nexus_health.py`.

Schema: `nexus-health/v2`

Statuses:
- PASS: local contract is satisfied.
- BLOCKED: capability is intentionally unavailable until its activation gates are satisfied.
- READY: activation gates currently permit the capability.
- FAIL: an expected safety/runtime invariant is broken.

Diagnostics:
- BLOCKED checks include a machine-readable reason, severity, action, and next step.
- Diagnostics explain the activation condition; they do not bypass approval or safety gates.
- Android Chrome remains dependent on an authorized CDP endpoint and explicit approval.

The report is deterministic and does not perform network, browser, credential, Android Chrome, or repository-write operations.

Google Cloud Build generates `health/nexus-health.json` and `health/nexus-health.html` during validation. These are build artifacts; their presence alone is not evidence that the build succeeded. Review the build result and report contents together.
