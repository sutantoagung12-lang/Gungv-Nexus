# Growth Runtime

Nexus can support continuous improvement and monetization research without making growth itself an authority.

## Growth loop

**Opportunity → Evidence → Cost/Risk → Experiment → Evaluation → Revenue signal → Lesson → Next experiment**

The opportunity engine estimates a transparent score from:
- expected value
- cost
- risk
- confidence

It does not guarantee profit or predict returns.

## Continuity

Research preparation and checkpoints support long-running work. External data remains untrusted until validated.

## Human authority

The user chooses which opportunity to pursue. Destructive, financial, account, publishing or external-world actions require explicit execution adapters and appropriate approval.
